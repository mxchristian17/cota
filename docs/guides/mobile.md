# Mobile view

The phone view for the job site.

On a phone Cota opens a reduced view made for the field (tablets and computers keep the full editor):

- **⋮ ▸ Open drawing…** opens a `.cota`, `.dxf` or `.dwg` from the phone (with the server and signed in, the list of
  your drawings). The selector at the top switches between the model and the layouts.
- **One finger** orbits a 3D part (in a 2D drawing and on a layout it moves the view; the round button changes it).
  **Two fingers** pinch to zoom and move the view. A **double tap** shows everything.
- **Fit**, **Views** (top, front, right, isometrics…) and **Style** (2D wireframe, wireframe, hidden, shaded,
  shaded with edges).
- **Layers** turns layers off only on the screen: the drawing does not change.
- **Measure** runs DIST: press, drag the ring that sits above your finger onto the point (object snap catches ends,
  midpoints and centers) and lift; then the second point. The card shows the distance, ΔX, ΔY, ΔZ and the angle,
  and the measured segment stays drawn until you close it.
- **⋮ ▸ Full editor** goes to the complete editor and **MOBILEVIEW** (View menu) comes back; the choice is saved with
  your preferences. `?mobile=1` in the address forces the phone view in that tab.

In the full editor on a tablet, two fingers pinch and move the view and one finger works as the mouse.

The full editor also works on a phone, fitted to the width of the screen: the menus go on a second row (swipe them
sideways), the command prompt wraps instead of pushing the input off the screen, and the Properties palette and the
agent panel open over the drawing (the agent starts closed). The **📱 Mobile view** button, at the bottom right of the
drawing, goes back to the phone view; if you zoom the page with two fingers it stays on screen.
