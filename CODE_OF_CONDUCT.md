# Code of Conduct

Cota adopts the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/)
as its code of conduct.

In short: be welcoming, patient and respectful; accept constructive criticism gracefully; focus on what is best
for the community. Harassment, insults, personal or political attacks, and publishing other people's private
information are not acceptable, in any project space or when representing the project.

## Reporting

Report unacceptable behavior privately to the maintainer,
[Christian Orengia (@mxchristian17)](https://github.com/mxchristian17). All reports are reviewed promptly and
handled confidentially. The maintainer may remove, edit or reject comments, commits, code, issues and other
contributions that do not follow this code, and may ban contributors temporarily or permanently, following the
enforcement guidelines of the Contributor Covenant.
